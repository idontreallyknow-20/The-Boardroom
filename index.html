Model Narrative

TrustLayer's revenue model combines per-word pricing with an annual platform retainer. As Nortam scales from large law firms to smaller regulated accounts, average revenue per customer falls, but total ARR grows because the product becomes more standardized and the self-serve Tier 1 path absorbs lower-volume buyers.

Sensitivity Note

This simulator is not a forecast. It is a sensitivity model that shows how TrustLayer's economics change when customer mix, discounting, and tier usage assumptions change.

Methodology

Formulas and conventions behind every KPI on this page.

Show formulas

1. Per-customer revenue (per segment)

revenue = words × Σ(tier_share × tier_price) × (1 − discount) + retainer × (1 − discount)

The blended price per word is the dot product of the segment's tier mix and the per-tier prices. Both the per-word stream and the annual platform retainer are discounted; direct cost is not discounted, so margin compresses as discount rises.

2. Per-customer direct cost

direct_cost = words × Σ(tier_share × tier_cost)

3. Contribution per customer

contribution = revenue − direct_cost. This is the dollar amount each customer contributes toward fixed costs and CAC. It is the denominator of break-even and the numerator of CAC payback.

4. Gross margin

gross_margin = (total_revenue − total_direct_cost) / total_revenue, weighted across the customer mix.

5. CAC payback (months)

cac_payback = loaded_CAC / (avg_contribution / 12). CAC tiers by total customer count: $22,500 for the first 10, $14,500 for 11 to 100, $6,000 for 101 to 1,000. All three are editable.

6. Break-even customers

break_even = fixed_cost / avg_contribution_per_customer. Operational uses $1.7M, fully loaded uses $2.9M. Both round up to the next integer customer.

7. Scale chart

The 10 / 100 / 1,000 ARR chart recomputes total ARR under the current mix, tier, retainer, and discount assumptions at each of those customer counts. The line is intentionally linear at constant mix; in practice the mix would shift toward smaller accounts at higher scale.
