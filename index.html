01

Looking accurate is not the same as being accountable.

Clients can now get a translation from an AI tool in seconds for almost nothing. For a disclosure, a clinical label or a merger filing, one wrong clause is a legal problem, and the AI tool cannot say who checked it.

In force now

Quebec Bill 96

Companies must show their French versions are correct, not just present.

May 2027

OSFI Guideline E-23

Banks and insurers become answerable for every AI model they rely on, including a vendor's translator.

How does Nortam get the speed and cost of AI without giving up the trust its reputation was built on?
02

Assess, route, certify.

Low-risk work gets the speed of AI. High-risk work gets a certified linguist and legal sign-off. Nothing leaves without a record.

A

Assess

A classifier scores each document on content type, jurisdiction and cost of error, using the categories Nortam already quotes with.

R

Route

The score picks one of three tiers. The tier sets how much human review it gets and the price per word.

C

Certify

The translation ships with a TrustCertificate: risk level, review path, who signed off, and a fingerprint of the file.
