Formulas

Every number on this page comes from these.

Show the math

Revenue per customer

words × Σ(tier share × tier price) × (1 − discount) + retainer × (1 − discount)

Blended price is the tier mix dotted with tier prices. Discount hits both streams.

Direct cost per customer

words × Σ(tier share × tier cost)

Cost is never discounted, so margin compresses as discount rises.

Contribution

revenue − direct cost

What each customer pays toward fixed cost and CAC.

Gross margin

(total revenue − total direct cost) / total revenue

Weighted across the customer mix.

CAC payback

loaded CAC / (contribution / 12)

CAC bracket is set by total customer count: 1 to 10, 11 to 100, 101 to 1,000.

Break-even

fixed cost / contribution per customer

$1.7M ongoing, or $2.9M including the build. Rounded up to a whole customer.
